# ICS-43434 lifecycle check and microphone fallback

The ICS-43434 chip chosen in ADR-0017 is end-of-life, but its breakout is still in stock, so the
C1 rig keeps it, buys spares now and records the TDK T5848 as the fallback for later units.

- Date: 2026-11-02 (issue #84, brick C1)
- Decision owner: the project owner (option A validated 2026-11-02)
- Related: [ADR-0016](../decisions/ADR-0016-acoustic-axis-collect-then-gate.md),
  [ADR-0017 § 3 Microphones](../decisions/ADR-0017-capture-rig-hardware.md),
  [spec capture-rig](../architecture/specs/capture-rig.md)

## Findings (checked 2026-11-02)

| Source | Status |
|---|---|
| TDK InvenSense product page | ICS-43434 listed as EOL; TDK product search: Obsolete |
| TDK PCN-000772 (2026-01-15) | Discontinued; last-time buy 2026-06-15 (passed), last-time ship 2027-01-15; alternates named: T5837, T5838, T5848 |
| DigiKey | Chip: Obsolete; evaluation board EV_ICS-43434-FX: Obsolete |
| Mouser | Chip: End of Life, backorder only |
| Octopart | NRND (stale record, same dates as the PCN) |
| Adafruit 6049 breakout | Still sold (8.95 USD); Mouser ~900 in stock with more on order (snapshot), Electrokit and The Pi Hut list it |
| Adafruit blog, 2026-03-09 | "The Great Search" for an ICS-43434 replacement; suggests the Knowles SPH0645LM4H |

Fallback candidates:

- **TDK T5848** (official alternate, I²S, Active, 16-week lead time): supply and I/O at
  1.62–1.98 V only, so the Pi 5 3.3 V GPIO needs a level shifter; frame format depends on the
  power mode; no mainstream breakout (one ESP32-oriented module from PCB Cupid). Not drop-in.
- **T5837 / T5838**: PDM, not I²S; would need a different Pi 5 audio path. Not drop-in.
- **Knowles SPH0645LM4H** (Adafruit 3421): I²S and pin-compatible on the breakout, but the
  chip is marked obsolete at Mouser and DigiKey (records conflict) and has the known DC offset
  and low-end roll-off already scored in ADR-0017. Its successor SPH0655LM4H is PDM.

## Decision matrix

Totals and sensitivity are computed by
[`mcda/research_2026_11_02_microphone_lifecycle.py`](../decisions/mcda/research_2026_11_02_microphone_lifecycle.py).
Criteria: wingbeat band fidelity 30 · available now for C1 25 · longevity 15 · Pi 5 integration 20
· cost 10.

| Option | Band | Now | Longevity | Pi 5 | Cost | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Keep ICS-43434 (Adafruit 6049) + spares** | 4 — unchanged from ADR-0017 | 5 — breakout in stock | 1 — chip EOL | 5 — proven I²S breakout, 3.3 V logic | 5 | **410** |
| B. SPH0645 (Adafruit 3421) | 3 — DC offset, roll-off | 3 — stock varies | 1 — chip obsolete | 5 | 5 | 330 |
| C. T5848 + 1.8 V level shifter | 4 — 68 dBA SNR | 2 — chip only, no breakout | 5 — active | 1 — custom board, mode-dependent framing | 2 | 285 |

Sensitivity: longevity 35 (band 25, now 15, Pi 5 15, cost 10) → **C 340 > A 335 (flips)**;
Pi 5 integration 35 → A 410, B 360, C 270; band 40 → A 420. A wins while the need is the C1
prototype; once longevity dominates (a fleet for M6), C wins.

## Consequences

- ADR-0017 stands: no supersession needed.
- **Order 4 × Adafruit 6049** (2 used + 2 spares) with the bench order, before stock runs out.
  BOM line 7 of ADR-0017 becomes qty 4: +24 € → unit 1 ≈ 369 €.
- Before any build beyond the two C1 units (M6 fleet), re-check stock and open an ADR for the
  T5848 (or a newer I²S part) with a 1.8 V level-shifter board.
- Order list for the bench Pi 5: separate issue.
