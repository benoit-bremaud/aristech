## What is good and must be kept

- The dependency rule holds: `domain/` imports only stdlib, `application/` only `domain`, heavy libraries confined to `infrastructure/` and a lazy import in `evaluation/runner.py:33`.
- The three real ports (`VideoSource`, `HornetDetector`, `Notifier`) each have a present force (fakes in tests, second consumer in `evaluation/`, optional extras).
- `evaluation/` is correctly placed and well documented; `decide()` is a pure function with the legal reasoning in its docstring.
- Unit tests use hand-written fakes at the ports, never mocks of the domain; the pyramid is healthy (72 unit / 9 integration / 0 e2e).
- `docs/INDEX.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `requirements.md` and `PROJECT_LOG.md` are clear and current on scope and decisions.
- No secret in the history.

## Disagree

- "Remove the ports layer entirely": no. Three ports are justified; only the five speculative ones go (S1).
- "Keep `training/` for M5": no. M5 is a VespAI fine-tune (YOLO), not Keras; the package is legacy.
